The commands of this guide, collected.

Chapter 3 — prove the install
    tesseract --version
    tesseract --list-langs

Chapter 4 — the first read
    tesseract images/notice-clean.png stdout
    tesseract images/notice-clean.png results/notice-clean
    python3 measure.py images/notice-clean.txt results/notice-clean.txt

Chapter 5 — repairs live in fix-images.py; reads after repair
    tesseract images/notice-skewed8-fixed.png stdout
    tesseract images/notice-noisy2-fixed.png stdout
    tesseract images/notice-lowres2-fixed.png stdout

Chapter 6 — segmentation and orientation
    tesseract images/columns.png stdout            (default, --psm 3)
    tesseract images/columns.png stdout --psm 6    (interleaved: the trap)
    tesseract images/notice-upside-down.png stdout --psm 0   (report only)
    tesseract images/notice-upside-down.png stdout --psm 1   (read upright)

Chapter 7 — languages
    tesseract images/letter-deu.png stdout -l eng
    tesseract images/letter-deu.png stdout -l deu
    tesseract images/letter-deu.png stdout -l eng+deu

Chapter 8 — output formats (config names)
    tesseract images/notice-clean.png out txt
    tesseract images/notice-clean.png out tsv
    tesseract images/notice-clean.png out hocr
    tesseract images/notice-clean.png out pdf
    pdftotext out.pdf - | head

Chapter 9 — the doubtful words
    awk -F'\t' '$11 < 60 && $12 != "" {print $12, "("$11")"}' page.tsv

Chapter 10 — batch and pipes
    for f in batch-demo/*.png; do tesseract "$f" "${f%.png}"; done
    tesseract pages-list.txt combined
    cat images/notice-clean.png | tesseract stdin stdout

Every command above was run in the guide's lab; the transcripts
are in ../tesseract-lab/transcripts/ and the accuracy ledger is
t90-accuracy-ledger.txt there.
